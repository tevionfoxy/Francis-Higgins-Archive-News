TRANSCRIPTION ACCURACY

Fewer invented lines, everywhere

Whisper's failure mode on long streams is to loop. The same short phrase gets written down hundreds of times over silence, music or game audio. The measure used throughout is the share of lines that repeat an earlier line exactly, and the longest unbroken run of identical lines.

What the old model was making up

2026-09-15 (2) - More misery

One flight-simulator radio phrase and the number after it were written 1,151 times each, in strict alternation, between minutes 120 and 160. Parakeet has ordinary speech there instead.

2025-04-15 (2) - Verdansk '25

The old transcript ran to 284 minutes. The video is 3 hours 45 minutes, so the last 59 minutes of that transcript described nothing that exists.

2021-08-13 - Shlug ~ Life

"Thank you." written 149 times over roughly an hour of silence.

2023-06-04 (1) - Wash and Wax

Ended with "Transcription by CastingWords" repeated 30 times, a well-known Whisper artifact.

Word count fell 11% overall (15.1M to 13.5M). That is mostly the invented filler being removed, so a lower count here is the intended result. The measures count lines, not correctness, and a real recurring bit (a soundboard clip, a repeated joke) can legitimately repeat, which is why the after figure is not zero.
